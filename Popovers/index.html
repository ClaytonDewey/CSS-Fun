Popovers: Light, Airy, and Criminally Underrated

Somewhere between a Yorkshire pudding and a dinner roll lives the popover, a hollow vessel of pure potential. Bakers have long overlooked it in favour of flashier breads, yet nothing else at the table rises so dramatically from so little.
