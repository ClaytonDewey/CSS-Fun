The Longhand Society

A writing club for people who still believe a sentence is worth getting right.

Get prompts, session notes, and occasional essays in your inbox.

We meet, we write, we read aloud.

Every second Thursday, a small group of writers gathers in a back room above a bookshop on Grafton Street. We spend the first hour on a prompt, the second reading what we made. No workshopping, no grades. Just the work and the room.

Membership is open. You do not need to have published anything. You need to want to write.

When
Every second Thursday, 7pm
Where
The Attic, Broderick Books, 14 Grafton Street
Cost
Free. Tea and biscuits provided.
